About This Site

An independent, fan-made guide and play portal for Azeroth Survivors.

What PlayAzerothSurvivors.com is

A small, independent fan project with one job: get you from “I heard about this game” to “I'm playing” fast — then keep you playing with clear guides. We cover the play portal, beginner walkthroughs, class builds and tier rankings, downloads, and the loading-and-error fixes people actually search for.

How we source facts

  • Version-stamped: every version-dependent claim carries the game version and the date we checked it — currently v0.143.0, 2026-11-10.
  • Credited opinions: tier rankings and build opinions are compiled from the period's dedicated guide sites and player videos, and labeled as opinions — the game ships no official tier list.
  • Official links only: play and download links point exclusively to the developer's official site, wow.aethus.pl. We never host game files.
  • No borrowed art: our diagrams, class icons and backgrounds are self-drawn CSS and SVG — no game screenshots or Blizzard assets.
