Quick answers

Azeroth Survivor — the four questions everyone asks

What is it?

Azeroth Survivor — almost always written Azeroth Survivors — is a free, fan-made survivors-style roguelike set in the look and spells of World of Warcraft Classic (client 1.12). You move with WASD, your spells cast themselves, and each zone ends with a scheduled final boss.

Who made it?

A developer known as Aethus, who publishes it at wow.aethus.pl ↗. It is a one-developer fan project backed by a huge community — the official Discord counts 113,690 members (public Discord API, 2026-11-10).

Is it free?

Yes — no payment and no account to play in the browser (checked 2026-11-10); the only money-adjacent thing is an optional Buy-me-a-coffee link on the hero picker. No Steam page exists right now.

What's the WoW connection?

The game borrows WoW Classic's classes, spells and zone names — but it is not an official Blizzard game, needs no Blizzard account, and Azeroth / World of Warcraft remain trademarks of Blizzard Entertainment, Inc.

What kind of game is Azeroth Survivor?

A survivors-style roguelike — the genre Vampire Survivors made famous — with a World of Warcraft skin that fans immediately recognize. You pick one of 9 classes (Hunter, Warlock, Mage, Paladin, Warrior, Shaman, Priest, Druid, Rogue) and one of 8 races, drop into one of 13 zones, and survive waves of monsters while your spells fire on their own. Every level-up deals you three loot-colored cards — a new spell, a higher rank, or a passive buff — and bosses arrive on a schedule, enraging below 30% health until the zone's final boss (Elwynn Forest's Edwin VanCleef arrives at 16:00) ends the run or opens endless mode.

Who is behind it?

  • Developer: Aethus — individual developer; official site wow.aethus.pl/ with updates and bug reports on the official Discord.
  • Community: 113,690 members / 33,363 online on the official Discord at peak (public Discord API, measured 2026-11-10 13:4x).
  • Open-source engine port: OutThisLife/azeroth-survivors-re on GitHub — a from-scratch WoW 1.12.1 client engine in Rust + Bevy, Apache-2.0 licensed, compiled to WebAssembly (README + GitHub API, 2026-11-10).
  • Breakout: October 2026 — the game trended on YouTube and Reddit in the second half of the month.

What does it cost?

Nothing. The browser version runs free with no account (an optional Buy-me-a-coffee support link sits on the hero picker), and the official Windows client is likewise a free download linked from the official site. There is no Steam page at the time of writing (2026-11-10, game v0.143.0).

How does it relate to World of Warcraft?

It is a fan-made homage, not a Blizzard product: the spells, classes and zones echo WoW Classic's 1.12 era — which is exactly why players call it WoW Survivors — but it ships as a self-contained browser or Windows game. You never log in with a Blizzard account, and no World of Warcraft installation or subscription is involved. Legally, Azeroth and World of Warcraft are trademarks of Blizzard Entertainment, Inc.; this site and the game are both unofficial fan works.

FAQ

Azeroth Survivor FAQ

Is Azeroth Survivor the same as Azeroth Survivors?

Yes — Azeroth Survivor is simply the singular form people type when searching, and it is the top related search of the game's actual name, Azeroth Survivors. Both refer to the same free browser roguelike by Aethus.

How do you spell it — Azeroth Survivors?

Correct spelling: Azeroth Survivors — two words, and that's the form we use site-wide. It's often misspelled as azertoh survivor, azeroth survivers or azeroth surviors; all of those searches lead to this same game.

What kind of game is it?

A survivors-style roguelike: your character's spells cast themselves while you steer with WASD, dodge hordes, draft one of three loot-colored upgrade cards on every level-up, and beat a boss schedule that ends each zone with a final boss.

Who is the developer?

A developer known as Aethus. The game is published at wow.aethus.pl, and its official Discord counts 113,690 members (public Discord API, checked 2026-11-10). An open-source Rust + Bevy engine port (Apache-2.0) exists on GitHub (OutThisLife/azeroth-survivors-re).

Is Azeroth Survivor free to play?

Yes. The browser version asks for no payment and no account (checked 2026-11-10); the hero picker only shows an optional Buy-me-a-coffee support link. There is no Steam page at the time of writing.

What does it have to do with World of Warcraft?

It borrows the look and spells of WoW Classic (the 1.12 client era) — classes like Paladin, Warlock and Mage, and zone names like Elwynn Forest. But it is a fan project: not made or endorsed by Blizzard, and you do not need a WoW account or subscription to play it.

Is it safe and official?

The game is safe to play from its official site, wow.aethus.pl — no payment, no account, no install for the browser version. It is not an official Blizzard product; Azeroth and World of Warcraft are trademarks of Blizzard Entertainment, Inc.
