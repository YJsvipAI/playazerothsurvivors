Azeroth Survivors Game

What it is, how fast it moves, and the three official ways to play it.

Play Free Online ↗ Download guide

Azeroth Survivors is the free browser roguelite by developer Aethus: survivors-style runs wrapped in World of Warcraft Classic's 1.12-era look and spells. The browser build runs on a Rust + Bevy engine compiled to WebAssembly, needs no account, and patches at a pace most games reserve for years — a dozen versions landed in October 2026 alone.

Version history

Version timeline: v0.87 → v0.143.0

The game patches fast — v0.114 to v0.142 landed inside one month in October 2026. Each milestone below carries its source; the current version is v0.143.0 (2026-11-10).

  • v0.87 Early release showcased in the first wave of gameplay videos (Noobheal HQ upload, Oct 9, 2026 — video caption “Game 0.87 initial release showcase”).
  • v0.90 Early WebGPU browser era (Ghost Gaming Insider walkthrough, Oct 11, 2026 — caption “Game 0.90 early WebGPU browser overview”).
  • v0.91.x Early-November guide sites' screenshots still reference v0.91.2 — the era before the October update storm (fan-wiki screenshot note, read 2026-11-10).
  • v0.114.0 Co-op arrives: up to 5 players share one run, in the browser version (azerothsurvivorsgame.com FAQ, game 0.142.0 text).
  • v0.142.0 The version current guides document in depth: 13 zones, 5-player co-op, 51-point talent trees (guide-site banner “Current game 0.142.0 features 13 zones, 5-player co-op, and 51-pt talent trees”, read 2026-11-10).
  • v0.143.0 Current version as of 2026-11-10 (community patch tracking and guide banners; re-verified 2026-11-10).

Older intermediate versions (v0.92–v0.113 etc.) were not individually documented by the guide sites we checked; the fan wiki's patch-notes section is the place that fills those gaps. We only list milestones with a source.

Is it free?

Yes on every channel: the browser version asks for no payment and no account, the hero picker only offers an optional Buy-me-a-coffee support link, and the official Windows client is a free download from the official site. There is no Steam page at the time of writing (2026-11-10, game v0.143.0). If any site asks you to pay for Azeroth Survivors, it is not the official one.

FAQ

Azeroth Survivors game FAQ

What is the Azeroth Survivors game?

A free fan-made survivors-style roguelike built on the look and spells of World of Warcraft Classic (1.12). It runs on a Rust + Bevy engine compiled to WebAssembly in the browser, with an official Windows client as the second official channel.

What is the latest version?

v0.143.0 as of 2026-11-10. The game patched rapidly through October 2026 — v0.114 to v0.142 landed within about a month — and guide sites re-check version-dependent facts each patch.

Where can I play it?

In the browser at the official site wow.aethus.pl (needs WebGPU), or via the official Windows client linked from that same site. Both are free and need no account. There is no Steam page at the time of writing.

When did multiplayer arrive?

Co-op for up to 5 players in one run was added in game v0.114.0 and works in the browser version.

How much has the game changed since launch?

A lot: v0.87 was the early release showcased on YouTube on Oct 9, 2026; by v0.142.0 the game had 13 zones, 5-player co-op and 51-point talent trees. Updates kept landing through early November, with v0.143.0 current on 2026-11-10.

Is the game related to Blizzard?

No — it is a fan project by Aethus using WoW-inspired art direction and spells. Azeroth and World of Warcraft are trademarks of Blizzard Entertainment, Inc., and no Blizzard account is needed to play.
