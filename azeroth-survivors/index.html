Azeroth Survivors

The free survivors-style roguelike in the looks and spells of WoW Classic.

Play Free Online Read the Beginner Guide

Azeroth Survivors is a free fan-made survivors-style roguelike by developer Aethus: runs wrapped in World of Warcraft Classic's 1.12-era art direction and spells, playable in your browser with no account and no download. It is not an official Blizzard game — it is an independent fan project that has caught fire with WoW players who want a quick survivors-style fix.

The game itself

What Azeroth Survivors is

The formula is the survivors-style one: hordes swarm you, your abilities fire automatically, and every level-up asks you to draft one of three loot-colored upgrade cards until your hero is a walking apocalypse. The twist is the setting — nine Warcraft-flavored classes (Hunter, Warlock, Mage, Paladin, Warrior, Shaman, Priest, Druid, Rogue), thirteen zones unlocking in order from Elwynn Forest to Naxxramas, and a scheduled final boss closing each zone.

The browser build runs on a Rust + Bevy engine compiled to WebAssembly and needs a WebGPU-capable browser (current Chrome, Edge or Safari). An official Windows client is the second channel. The game patches fast: v0.114 to v0.142 landed inside one month in October 2026, with v0.143.0 current as of 2026-11-10 — our patch notes and version history tracks the milestones with sources.

FAQ

Azeroth Survivors FAQ

What is Azeroth Survivors?

Azeroth Survivors is a free, fan-made survivors-style roguelike set in the looks and spells of World of Warcraft Classic (client 1.12), made by a developer known as Aethus. You pick one of 9 classes, move with WASD while your spells cast themselves, and draft one of three upgrade cards on every level-up. It is not an official Blizzard game, and it is free to play in your browser with no account.

How do you play Azeroth Survivors?

Move with WASD — your abilities fire automatically at nearby enemies. Each level-up offers a choice of three loot-colored upgrade cards, and each of the thirteen zones (Elwynn Forest through Naxxramas) ends with a scheduled final boss: in Elwynn Forest, Edwin VanCleef arrives at 16:00. After the final boss you can keep going in endless mode. Since game v0.114.0, up to 5 players can share one run in co-op.

Is Azeroth Survivors an official Blizzard game?

No. It is a fan project by Aethus using WoW-inspired art direction and spells. Azeroth and World of Warcraft are trademarks of Blizzard Entertainment, Inc., and the game is not made, endorsed or sponsored by Blizzard. You do not need a Blizzard account or a WoW subscription to play.
