The short answer

For most players right now: Hunter, with Warlock as the self-healing alternative and Mage for the fastest (riskiest) clears. That trio is the current S tier on game v0.142.0. Brand new? Start with Paladin or Warrior — the easiest classes to finish a zone with — then climb to the S tier once the loop clicks.

FAQ

Best class FAQ

What is the best class in Azeroth Survivors?

For most players right now: Hunter — pet-soaked safety with the scariest single-target crits in the game. Warlock is the self-sustaining alternative; Mage is the fastest but most fragile. (Ranking on game v0.142.0, checked 2026-11-10.)

What is the best class for beginners?

Paladin or Warrior — community guides name both as the easiest classes to finish a zone with, thanks to simple, durable melee kits.

Is Mage good despite low health?

Yes if you can position: Mage has the fastest screen-clear in the game and three very different capstones, but the least health and no pets — mistakes cost you the run.

Where do I find each class's full build?

Our builds hub carries every class's tier, role and play style, and per-class build pages (talent priorities, capstones, zone fit) are the next content wave. Tier reasons are on the tier list.
