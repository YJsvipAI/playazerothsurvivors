Talent tree

Hunter talent recommendations, in three layers

Every class grows one talent tree between runs — roughly 39–40 talents, 51 points total, a new row every 5 points, with a milestone at 10 and 20 points and a single capstone slot at 30 (one capstone per run; respecs are free). The Hunter path below is the crit-sniper standard.

  • Capstone · 30 ptsKill Shot Starts your run with Aimed Shot, cuts its cooldown 15%, and Aimed Shot resets on every kill (up to once every 2 seconds). This is the engine of the “100k crit” sniper build.
    • Alt — Bestial Wrath: the Beast pack build; safest way to learn boss patterns while pets do heavy lifting.
    • Alt — Wyvern Sting: trap build; starts with Frost Trap and drops an Explosive Trap under you every 5 seconds.
  • Milestones · 10 / 20 ptsRapid Fire (MM 20) · Tame Beast + Frenzy (BM 10/20) Priority picks: Rapid Fire sends a free Aimed Shot every 6 shots; Tame Beast & Frenzy give your wolf a 75% chance to bite out a free Multi-Shot.
    • MM line: Piercing Shots (10) → Rapid Fire (20)
    • BM line: Tame Beast (10) → Frenzy (20)
    • Survival line: Explosive Ammunition (20)
  • Path passivesCrit chance · crit damage · all damage · cooldowns Lethal Shots, Mortal Shots, Ranged Weapon Specialization, Ferocity and friends — the crit-sniper stack that turns Aimed Shot resets into one-shot waves.
Level-up draft

Core skills & pick priority

Every level-up offers three loot-colored cards. Spend them roughly in this order:

  • Auto Shot first — your always-on starter; early ranks alone clear the first waves.
  • Multi-Shot and Volley next — widen Auto Shot into screen-clearing area damage (editor's note, consistent with the .net build direction).
  • Aimed Shot and Serpent Sting — your single-target answers for elites and scheduled bosses.
  • Crit and flat-damage passives — with the Kill Shot tree online, every kill can chain into a free Aimed Shot crit.
  • Explosive Trap is the insurance — it fires when something reaches melee range; Frost Trap sets up the Wyvern Sting trap build.

Core spells you will see: Auto Shot · Multi-Shot · Aimed Shot · Arcane Shot · Volley · Explosive Trap · Frost Trap (per azerothsurvivorsgame.com's Hunter spell list). Gear that loves this build: Band of Accuria, Master Dragonslayer's Ring, Hand of Justice, Gnomish Universal Remote, Amulet of Vek'nilash (+1 target), Onyxia Tooth Pendant — Tracker's through Cryptstalker sets, bows up to Rhok'delar.

Avoid: standing still against bosses — the whole Hunter kit assumes you keep moving.

Hero pick

Races & mobility for Hunter

  • Troll — Berserking: cooldowns recover 30% faster for 10 seconds; plus 3% crit and regeneration.
  • Dwarf — Stoneform shield plus gun specialization (+6% damage) and Treasure Finding (+30% pickup radius).
  • Defaults & unlocks — Dwarf, Night Elf, Orc, Tauren and Troll are available by default; since v0.120.0 achievement points unlock any race for any class (Rewards → Races on the title screen).
  • Mobility — Hunter's movement skill is Aspect of the Cheetah (from level 5, on F, steers with your mouse).

FAQ

Azeroth Survivors Hunter FAQ

Is Hunter good in Azeroth Survivors?

Yes — S tier on the current community ranking: #3 of 9 for ease and #1 of 9 for damage ceiling. It kills from a safe distance, pets add free damage, and it owns the scariest single-target crits in the game. (azerothsurvivorsgame.com, Hunter page measured on v0.130.1; ranking snapshot v0.142.0.)

What is the best Hunter build?

The Aimed Shot crit sniper: Kill Shot capstone → free-Aimed-Shot milestones → crit chance / flat damage passives → crit rings. Every kill can reset Aimed Shot, so dense waves chain into huge crits. The Beast pack build (Bestial Wrath) is the safest alternative while you learn boss patterns.

What are the Hunter capstone talents?

Kill Shot, Bestial Wrath and Wyvern Sting — one capstone per run at 30 talent points, and respecs are free between runs. (Talent trees per azerothsurvivorsgame.com and the azeroth-survivors.org Wiki.)

What is the best race for Hunter?

Troll (Berserking speeds cooldowns) or Dwarf (Stoneform shield + gun damage). Defaults are Dwarf, Night Elf, Orc, Tauren and Troll — and since v0.120.0 achievement points unlock any race for any class.
