S tier right now

The three S-tier builds

Hunter

Safe-distance damage, pets soaking free hits, the scariest single-target crits in the game — the best safety-to-power mix for most players.

Warlock

DoTs that jump corpse-to-corpus, demons fighting for you, self-healing from your own damage-over-time. Snowballs once talents come online.

Mage

Fastest screen-clear and three very different capstones — priced in the least health and no pets, so positioning is everything.

Mechanics

How builds actually come together

  • In-run drafts: every level-up offers three loot-colored cards — a new spell, a higher rank, or a passive buff. Builds are the sum of these picks plus what your class starts with.
  • Talent trees: every class grows a talent tree between runs — 51 points' worth at v0.142.0 — with milestones and capstones (Mage alone ships three very different capstones).
  • Gear & trinkets: rings, trinkets and amulets drop into the build mix — the guide sites count 23 Codex jewels, 29 amulets and 9 class Armory gear sets at v0.142.0.
  • Racials: Gnome luck, Orc Blood Fury, Undead cheat death — pick the race that covers your build's weak side.
  • Zones: 13 zones, unlock in order; melee builds pay more uptime tax the deeper you go — one reason ranged S tier holds.

FAQ

Builds FAQ

What is the best build in Azeroth Survivors right now?

On the current community ranking (game v0.142.0): Hunter for pet-soaked safety with huge crits, Warlock for self-healing DoT snowballs, Mage for the fastest screen-clear if you can play around its low health. Full reasons on our tier list.

How do builds work?

Your build comes from two places: the three-card draft on every level-up (new spells, higher ranks or passives) during a run, and the per-class talent tree — 51 points' worth at v0.142.0 — that you grow between runs with talent XP.

Do races change builds?

Racials matter at hero pick: Gnomes get luck, Orcs get Blood Fury, Undead cheat death. The class defines the build; the race nudges the margins.

Will there be a build page for every class?

Yes — and all nine are live: each class has its own build page (talent priorities, capstone picks, skill draft order, zone fit and FAQ) written against game v0.143.0. Start from the class cards on this hub.
