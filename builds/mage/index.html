Talent tree

Mage talent recommendations, in three layers

Every class grows one talent tree between runs — roughly 39–40 talents, 51 points total, a new row every 5 points, a milestone at 10 and 20 points and one capstone slot at 30 (one capstone per run; respecs are free). The Mage ships three genuinely different capstones.

  • Capstone · 30 ptsPyroclasm Starts your run with Pyroblast; fire kills detonate the next enemy for 25% of its max HP, up to 3 times per second — the full-screen chain-explosion build.
    • Alt — Summon Water Elemental: frost control; starts with Frostbolt, the elemental tanks for you and casts a free Frost Nova every 8 seconds. Recommended for a first Mage run.
    • Alt — Arcane Power: starts with Arcane Missiles; spells have a 20% chance to echo, and echoes can re-trigger up to 3 times.
  • Milestones · 10 / 20 ptsIgnite (10) → Hot Streak (20) The Fire line is the Pyroclasm partner: ignite spreads burn damage and hot streak hands out free Pyroblasts on crits.
    • Frost line: Frostbite (10) → Splitting Ice (20)
    • Arcane line: Arcane Concentration (10) → Presence of Mind (20)
  • Path passivesCrit chance · ignite · all damage Crit chance feeds Hot Streak and Pyroclasm at once — crit and ignite passives are the standard third-layer picks on the fire-chain build.
Level-up draft

Core skills & pick priority

Every level-up offers three loot-colored cards. Spend them roughly in this order:

  • Fireball first — your starter; maxing the first spell early is the classic Mage draft logic.
  • Blizzard, Flamestrike or Arcane Explosion second — pick the area shape your first spell is missing.
  • Pyroblast and the fire-chain passives — the Pyroclasm snowball needs fuel: crit chance, ignite, all damage.
  • A defensive card earlier than feels necessary — Glass cannon math: one bad surround ends the run, and Ice Barrier or a health card is cheaper than a lost run (the .net build direction says exactly this).
  • Blink discipline — Blink is your entire defence in the deep zones; keep the lane you blink into clean.

Core spells you will see: Fireball · Frostbolt · Arcane Missiles · Frost Nova · Arcane Explosion · Blizzard · Flamestrike · Fire Blast · Cone of Cold · Blast Wave · Pyroblast (Wiki pool of 13 — the largest in the game — plus Ice Barrier and Polymorph). Gear that loves this build: Malice Stone Pendant (+20% echo) and Hand of Justice for Arcane Power; Master Dragonslayer's Ring + Onyxia Tooth Pendant for Fire.

Avoid: skipping health and armor cards — one bad surround ends the run (azerothsurvivorsgame.com's own avoid note for Mage).

Zones and the Mage's fit

Hero pick

Races & mobility for Mage

  • Gnome — +20% luck, faster cooldowns, Escape Artist to break roots.
  • Undead — Will of the Forsaken: 4 seconds of immunity at death's door — a second life for a class that dies instantly.
  • Troll — Berserking: cooldowns recover 30% faster for 10 seconds; plus 3% crit.
  • Defaults & unlocks — Human, Gnome, Undead and Troll are available by default; since v0.120.0 achievement points unlock any race for any class.
  • Mobility — Mage's movement skill is Blink (from level 5, on F, steers with your mouse) — on this class it is a defensive spell.

FAQ

Azeroth Survivors Mage FAQ

Is Mage good in Azeroth Survivors?

Yes — S tier: #3 of 9 for damage ceiling and the fastest screen-clear in the game; but #8 of 9 for ease — a glass cannon. (azerothsurvivorsgame.com, Mage page measured on v0.142.0.)

What is the best Mage build?

Fire chain explosions with Pyroclasm: kills burst into the next enemy for 25% of its max HP. The frost control build (Summon Water Elemental) is the recommended alternative for a first Mage run.

What are the Mage capstone talents?

Pyroclasm, Summon Water Elemental and Arcane Power — one capstone per run, free respecs between runs. (azerothsurvivorsgame.com tree, points cross-checked on the Wiki.)

Why does my Mage die instantly?

Lowest practical survivability of the S tier: no pets, thin health pool. Take a defensive card earlier than feels necessary; Blink is your entire defence. (azerothsurvivorsgame.com's avoid note plus the .net build direction.)
