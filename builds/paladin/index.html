Talent tree

Paladin talent recommendations, in three layers

Every class grows one talent tree between runs — roughly 39–40 talents, 51 points total, a new row every 5 points, a milestone at 10 and 20 points and one capstone slot at 30 (one capstone per run; respecs are free). Tree points below follow the Wiki's v0.139.2 Paladin page (the newest dedicated source).

  • Capstone · 30 ptsDivine Storm Pairs with an Exorcism start — every Exorcism adds 20% damage to enemies within 5 yards. The damage-oriented capstone.
    • Alt — Hammer of the Righteous: the tank capstone — +15% max HP, spells deal 1% of your max HP, and breaking shields detonates holy light.
    • Alt — Light's Grace: an automatic Holy Wrath every 14 seconds.
  • Milestones · 10 / 20 ptsArdent Defender (Prot 20) The shield bastion keystone: a Hammer of Justice start, Consecration stacks an 8%-per-rank shield up to 30%, and holding a shield adds +20% damage.
    • Holy line: Divine Favor (10 — Holy Shock start, jumps to 2 targets) → Daybreak (20)
    • Protection line: Hallowed Ground (10) → Ardent Defender (20)
    • Retribution line: Seal of Command (10) → The Art of War (20 — crits cast a free Exorcism 15% of the time)
  • Key support talentsDivine Intervention · Fortitude · Mark of the Wild Divine Intervention is the signature: once per 90 seconds, a killing blow doesn't kill you. Fortitude and Mark of the Wild pad the health and armor your playstyle lives on.
    • Community reference trees: ~31 Prot / 13 Holy (Consecration tank, from a 10–25 video guide) and 31 Ret / 20 Holy (Exorcism crit, asbuilds.app/b/QTs3GIMlF3) — both linked from the Wiki's Paladin page.
Level-up draft

Core skills & pick priority

Every level-up offers three loot-colored cards. Spend them roughly in this order:

  • Consecration first — your damage lives on the ground you stand on; rank it up early and often.
  • Holy Shock next — the flexible hit-and-heal; Exorcism follows as your boss-facing damage.
  • Holy Wrath once waves split — it scales with how many enemies surround you.
  • A survival card every few levels — the .net build direction: Consecration first, slot a defensive card in between.
  • Sixth slot: Divine Shield — the classic finisher for the sixth spell slot (per the older .org draft order).
  • Wiki tip — fill your spell slots first, then banish (skip) spells you don't use so level-up cards keep feeding your two or three main spells.

Core spells you will see: Consecration · Hammer of Justice · Holy Shock · Exorcism · Judgement · Holy Wrath · Divine Shield (per azerothsurvivorsgame.com's spell list; the Wiki pool adds Hammer of Wrath). Gear that loves this build: Quick Strike Ring, Ring of Spell Power, Hand of Justice, Horn of the Warchief, Malice Stone Pendant, Medallion of Steadfast Might — Lightforge through Redemption sets, up to Ashbringer.

Avoid: kiting at range — your damage is the ground under your feet; stand in it.

Hero pick

Races & mobility for Paladin

  • Dwarf — Stoneform shield and gun specialization; the tankier pick.
  • Human — Perception burst plus bonus experience and gold; the faster-growing pick.
  • Only these two — Paladin is locked to Human and Dwarf (per both guide sites).
  • Mobility — Paladin's movement skill is Blessing of Freedom (from level 5, on F, steers with your mouse).

FAQ

Azeroth Survivors Paladin FAQ

Is Paladin good in Azeroth Survivors?

Both rankings are true and we list them side by side: A tier on the endgame strength list (v0.142.0), and S tier for a first clear — the Wiki community's “easiest class to finish a zone with”. Consecration underfoot, stacked shields, self-healing, and a death save every 90 seconds. The trade: mid-pack damage ceiling (#8 of 9).

What is the best Paladin build?

Shield bastion (beginner): Hammer of Justice start → Consecration shield milestones → Divine Intervention → Fortitude / Mark of the Wild. Exorcism crit (second run): Divine Storm capstone with The Art of War free-cast procs.

What are the Paladin capstone talents?

Light's Grace, Divine Storm and Hammer of the Righteous — one capstone per run, free respecs between runs. (Points per Wiki v0.139.2; a 0.143 in-game check of the tree is still on our to-do list.)

What is the best race for Paladin?

Paladin is only Human or Dwarf. Dwarf (Stoneform) tanks more; Human (XP and gold) grows faster.
