Talent tree

Shaman talent recommendations, in three layers

Every class grows one talent tree between runs — roughly 39–40 talents, 51 points total, a new row every 5 points, a milestone at 10 and 20 points and one capstone slot at 30 (one capstone per run; respecs are free). One warning: the Enhancement line was reworked between v0.128 and v0.142, so older guides name a different capstone.

  • Capstone · 30 ptsElemental Mastery Starts your run with Chain Lightning and casts a second automatic bolt every 3 seconds — jumps lose no damage, so the denser the wave, the harder it hits.
    • Alt — Stormbound Echoes (reworked Enhancement, 0.142): Stormstrike start; Enhancement triggers have a 50% chance to trigger twice, and 1% of your output comes back as self-healing. (Wiki v0.128 still listed Feral Spirit here — same slot, reworked.)
    • Alt — Totemic Wrath: the stationary build — Magma Totem start, an automatic Searing Totem every 14 seconds. Spectacular damage, low mobility.
  • Milestones · 10 / 20 ptsLightning Overload (10) → Elemental Fury (20) The Elemental line multiplies your bolts — overload procs plus fury's damage amp are the storm chain's core milestones.
    • Enhancement line: Windfury (10) → Maelstrom Weapon (20)
    • Restoration line: Call of the Elements (10) → Reincarnation (20)
  • Path passives & gearEcho and +1-target effects Echo procs and extra-target effects multiply chain hits — Hand of Justice, Malice Stone Pendant and Amulet of Vek'nilash stack with Elemental Mastery and Lightning Overload for screens full of forked lightning.
Level-up draft

Core skills & pick priority

Every level-up offers three loot-colored cards. Spend them roughly in this order:

  • Lightning Bolt to rank 3–4 first — the classic .org draft order: max your starter's early ranks before adding more spells.
  • Chain Lightning next — your density answer; jumps don't fall off in the current build.
  • Earth Shock — the burst button for enraged scheduled bosses.
  • One totem — Searing or Magma; the .net direction says drop it and walk away, and that target-count and duration cards pay double on totems.
  • Stop at six spells — the .org draft order ends at the sixth skill; deepen what works instead of widening.

Core spells you will see: Lightning Bolt · Chain Lightning · Earth Shock · Flame Shock · Frost Shock · Searing Totem · Magma Totem · Lightning Shield (Wiki pool plus Fire Nova, Healing Wave and Call of Flame). Gear that loves this build: Hand of Justice, Malice Stone Pendant, Amulet of Vek'nilash — the echo/target stack.

Avoid: spreading points across lightning, totems and melee at once — commit to one playstyle per run (the guide sites' own warning for Shaman).

Zones and the Shaman's fit

Zones unlock per class: win a zone with one class and the next zone opens for that class only. The 13-zone path every Shaman walks:

Hero pick

Races & mobility for Shaman

  • Troll — Berserking: cooldowns recover 30% faster for 10 seconds; plus 3% crit and regeneration.
  • Orc — Blood Fury: +35% damage for 12 seconds and +10% max health.
  • Tauren — War Stomp: 2-second area stun every 15 seconds and +15% max health.
  • Defaults & unlocks — Orc, Tauren and Troll are available by default; since v0.120.0 achievement points unlock any race for any class.
  • Mobility — Shaman's movement skill is Ghost Wolf (from level 5, on F, steers with your mouse).

FAQ

Azeroth Survivors Shaman FAQ

Is Shaman good in Azeroth Survivors?

Yes — A tier: #6 of 9 for ease, #5 of 9 for damage ceiling. Chain Lightning scales beautifully with dense waves, and the reworked Enhancement tree adds ferocious single-target burst. (azerothsurvivorsgame.com, Shaman page measured on v0.142.0.)

What is the best Shaman build?

The storm chain: Elemental Mastery capstone — no-falloff jumps, a free bolt every 3 seconds, Earth Shock burst for enraged bosses. The alternative is the Stormstrike proc melee from reworked Enhancement.

What are the Shaman capstone talents?

Elemental Mastery, Stormbound Echoes and Totemic Wrath. Version note: Enhancement was reworked between v0.128 and v0.142 — the Wiki's v0.128 tree called this slot Feral Spirit, game 0.142 calls it Stormbound Echoes. A 0.143 in-game check is still on our to-do list.

Totems or lightning for Shaman?

Totemic Wrath is spectacular stationary damage but low mobility; the storm chain has far higher mobility and is safer against agile heroic bosses. (The guide sites' own comparison.)
