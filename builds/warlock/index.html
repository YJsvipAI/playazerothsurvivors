Talent tree

Warlock talent recommendations, in three layers

Every class grows one talent tree between runs — roughly 39–40 talents, 51 points total, a new row every 5 points, a milestone at 10 and 20 points and one capstone slot at 30 (one capstone per run; respecs are free). The Warlock path below is the corpse-jumping plague spread.

  • Capstone · 30 ptsSeed of Corruption Starts your run with Corruption; enemies that die with DoTs on them explode for 3% of their max HP in a 6-yard burst. Dying waves chain into each other — the core of the build.
    • Alt — Pact of the Nathrezim: the demon-lord summon build (Wiki v0.128 named it Pact of the Annihilan — same capstone, renamed).
    • Alt — Butcher: fel-guard cleave build; mutually exclusive with the Nathrezim pact.
    • Alt — Rain of Chaos: a second falling firestorm that leaves slowing burning ground.
  • Milestones · 10 / 20 ptsNightfall (10) → Contagion (20) The Affliction pair is the corpse-jump engine: when a cursed target dies, its DoTs hop to the nearest enemy. Prioritize this line.
    • Demonology line: Soul Devourer (10, felhunter) or Soul Link (10, voidwalker — its shield was cut from 25% to 10% in v0.124.0), Legion Gate (20)
    • Destruction line: Shadow Cleave (10) → Conflagrate (20)
  • Path passivesSiphon Life · area · haste Siphon Life is step three of the standard build — your DoTs tick health back to you, which matters on the lowest health pool in the game. Then stack area and haste passives.
Level-up draft

Core skills & pick priority

Every level-up offers three loot-colored cards. Spend them roughly in this order:

  • Seed of Corruption capstone first (once the talent tree is up) — it turns every DoT kill into an explosion.
  • Shadow Bolt and Corruption early — your starter and your first DoT; classic draft order adds Curse of Agony and one direct strike next.
  • Rain of Fire for density — with Hellfire as the risky-in-close alternative; survival cards repay themselves fastest on Warlock's 165 health.
  • Siphon Life and area/haste passives — keep the spread rolling and yourself alive.
  • Late picks — Death Coil and Soulfire — burst answers for elites; Soulfire anchors the popular luck-based community build (editor's note).

Core spells you will see: Shadow Bolt · Corruption · Curse of Agony · Immolate · Searing Pain · Rain of Fire · Soulfire (per azerothsurvivorsgame.com's spell list; the Wiki pool adds Death Coil, Hellfire, Fear, Demonic Empowerment). Gear that loves this build: Seal of the Damned (+10% DoT tick speed), Quick Strike Ring + The Legionlord's Medallion for demon builds, Earthstrike, Hand of Justice, Choker of the Fire Lord — Dreadmist through Plagueheart sets, staffs up to Atiesh.

Avoid: grabbing direct-damage cards on sight — DoT spread is the win condition, not burst.
Where it shines

Zones and the Warlock's fit

Zones unlock per class: win a zone with one class and the next zone opens for that class only. The 13-zone path every Warlock walks:

  • Density is fuel — the DoT spread scales with how many enemies share a screen; Scarlet Monastery, Alterac Valley and Naxxramas waves snowball hardest (editor's note).
  • Early zones need care — 165 base health is the thinnest pool in the game, so play conservatively until Contagion and Siphon Life are online.

Hero pick

Races & mobility for Warlock

  • Gnome — +20% luck, faster cooldowns, and Escape Artist to break roots for 2 seconds.
  • Orc — Blood Fury: +35% damage for 12 seconds and +10% max health (welcome on this health pool).
  • Undead — Will of the Forsaken: 4 seconds of immunity at death's door, healing on kills, and 4% damage reduction.
  • Defaults & unlocks — Human, Gnome, Orc and Undead are available by default; since v0.120.0 achievement points unlock any race for any class.
  • Mobility — Warlock's movement skill is Howl of Terror (from level 5, on F, steers with your mouse).

FAQ

Azeroth Survivors Warlock FAQ

Is Warlock good in Azeroth Survivors?

Yes — S tier on the current community ranking: #4 of 9 for ease and #2 of 9 for damage ceiling. DoTs jump corpse to corpse, demons fight for you, and your own damage-over-time heals you. Once the talent tree comes online, it snowballs. (azerothsurvivorsgame.com, Warlock page measured on v0.141.0.)

What is the best Warlock build?

The plague spreader: Seed of Corruption capstone → DoT-jump milestones (Nightfall → Contagion) → Siphon Life → area + haste passives. A popular community alternative is Soulfire + luck — Gnome luck and Mark of the Chosen to fish rare cards, Soulfire for elites.

What are the Warlock capstone talents?

Seed of Corruption, Pact of the Nathrezim, Butcher and Rain of Chaos. The Nathrezim pact and Butcher are mutually exclusive; you wear one capstone per run. (Wiki v0.128 still called it Pact of the Annihilan — renamed in later builds.)

Why does my Warlock feel weak early?

165 base health — the lowest of the nine classes — plus DoTs that need time to tick. Push through the first waves; once the talents land, the snowball reverses the feeling fast. (Health per the Wiki; the early-game note mirrors the community build pages.)
