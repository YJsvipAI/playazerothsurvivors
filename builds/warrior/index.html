Talent tree

Warrior talent recommendations, in three layers

Every class grows one talent tree between runs — roughly 39–40 talents, 51 points total, a new row every 5 points, a milestone at 10 and 20 points and one capstone slot at 30 (one capstone per run; respecs are free). The community-favorite Warrior build is the eternal whirlwind.

  • Capstone · 30 ptsBladestorm Starts your run with Whirlwind and adds a free spin every 3 seconds — the perpetual blender the guide sites call the community's favorite warrior build.
    • Alt — Mortal Wounds: Mortal Strike start; execute-style hits refresh it up to once per second.
    • Alt — Titan: the shield-wall build — Shield Slam start, damage reduction up to 70%, and spells hit for 1.5% of your max HP.
  • Milestones · 10 / 20 ptsSweeping Strikes (Arms 20) The “strike twice” milestone the guide sites highlight — every swing hits a second enemy, doubling your cleave.
    • Arms line: Deep Wounds (10) → Sweeping Strikes (20)
    • Fury line: Rampage (10) → Flurry (20)
    • Protection line: Shockwave (10) → Fortified (20)
  • Path passivesFree Whirlwind on kill · area · haste Kills trigger more Whirlwinds — kills feed spins, spins make kills. Stack area and haste passives to keep the wheel turning.
Level-up draft

Core skills & pick priority

Every level-up offers three loot-colored cards. Spend them roughly in this order:

  • Cleave to rank 8 first — the .org draft order is blunt: max Cleave before anything else, add one nova-type spell, then go back to Cleave.
  • Whirlwind / Sweeping Strikes — the .net direction pairs Cleave with the spin kit for permanent area pressure.
  • Execute for elites — your single-target answer for scheduled bosses.
  • Passives in order — Blessing of Might → Fortitude → Amplify Magic; on Warrior, health and armor are damage stats because you are always in melee range.

Core skills you will see: Cleave · Heroic Strike · Mortal Strike · Whirlwind · Thunder Clap · Demoralizing Shout · Sweeping Strikes · Execute · Retaliation (the older .org table's nine skills, names current per the Wiki pool of 11). Gear that loves this build: Archimtiros' Ring, Star of Mystaria and Medallion of Steadfast Might for the Titan tank build; Master Dragonslayer's Ring + Onyxia Tooth Pendant for Bladestorm.

Avoid: going full offense with no armor or health — late waves hit hard, and melee uptime needs a defensive base.

Hero pick

Races & mobility for Warrior

  • Every race works — Warrior is the only class all eight races can play by default.
  • Orc — Blood Fury: +35% damage for 12 seconds and +10% max health.
  • Tauren — War Stomp: a 2-second area stun every 15 seconds and +15% max health.
  • Mobility — Warrior's movement skill is Charge (from level 5, on F, steers with your mouse) — gap-closer and escape in one.

FAQ

Azeroth Survivors Warrior FAQ

Is Warrior good in Azeroth Survivors?

Yes — A tier: #1 of 9 for ease (the easiest class in the game) and #4 for damage ceiling. Huge health and cleaving swings make it the most forgiving melee class. (azerothsurvivorsgame.com, Warrior page measured on v0.142.0.)

What is the best Warrior build?

The eternal whirlwind: Bladestorm capstone → strike-twice milestone (Sweeping Strikes) → free Whirlwind on kill → area + haste passives. The shield-wall alternative is Titan — up to 70% damage reduction.

What are the Warrior capstone talents?

Bladestorm, Mortal Wounds and Titan — one capstone per run, free respecs between runs. (azerothsurvivorsgame.com tree, points cross-checked on the Wiki.)

What is the best race for Warrior?

Every race can be a Warrior — the only class unlocked for all eight races by default. Best picks: Orc (Blood Fury), Tauren (War Stomp) or Human.
