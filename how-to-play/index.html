How to Play Azeroth Survivors

Your first run, your first class, and where your progress goes — in game v0.143.0 terms.

Open the official game ↗ Browse builds
Quick start

Your first three minutes

Open the game and pick a hero

Go to wow.aethus.pl ↗ in a current Chrome, Edge or Safari — the game draws with WebGPU — and press Play. Choose a race, gender, class and zone. Racials matter: Gnomes get luck, Orcs get Blood Fury, Undead cheat death.

Move, dodge, collect

Steer with WASD. Your spells cast themselves — your job is positioning: kite the horde inside your auto-cast range, pick up the experience wisps and gold the dead leave behind, and watch the top timeline for scheduled boss spawns.

Draft every level-up

Each level-up deals three loot-colored cards: new spells, higher ranks or passive buffs. Take what fills the gaps in your kit. Bosses enrage below 30% health; the final boss ends the zone — then endless mode opens, and the run pays talent XP to your class for between-run growth.

Pick a class

Choosing your first class

New player? Take Paladin or Warrior — the community's standard recommendation as the easiest classes to finish a zone with. Once the loop clicks, the current S tier (Hunter, Warlock, Mage) is where the ceiling is. Full ranking in our tier list; pick comparisons in best class.

Systems you'll meet

Controls & the rest of the UI

  • Movement: WASD (gamepad support is listed among the game's extras). Your abilities auto-cast — no action bars.
  • Rerolls: level-up drafts can be rerolled — spend them when all three cards miss your build.
  • Gold & caches: kills drop gold; caches are the between-wave treat — both feed the run loop documented in guide sites' basics.
  • Luck: a stat some racials (Gnome) and gear affect — it nudges the quality of what drops and drafts.
  • Slash commands & shortcuts: built-ins for save backup and quick actions, per the guide sites' commands reference.

System names follow the guide sites' “how to play” basics for game 0.142.0 (controls, gold, caches, rerolls, luck, talents, endless mode); mechanical details stay as the game documents them.

Progress

Where your saves live

Every run pays talent experience to your class, so progress persists even when a run ends badly. Between runs you unlock talent milestones, capstones, gear, zones and achievements. The game also ships save-file backup through built-in slash commands and shortcuts — worth using, because a browser save lives in that browser's profile for the site you play on, and clearing the site's data wipes it.

FAQ

Beginner FAQ

How do I start Azeroth Survivors?

Open the official site wow.aethus.pl in a current Chrome, Edge or Safari (the game needs WebGPU), press Play, pick a race, class and zone, and move with WASD. Your spells cast themselves from there.

What is the easiest class for beginners?

Paladin or Warrior — community guides recommend both as the easiest classes to finish a zone with. Once you know the loop, Hunter, Warlock and Mage are the current S-tier picks.

How do I unlock more zones?

Zones unlock in order from Elwynn Forest (01) to Naxxramas (13) as you progress, and every run pays talent experience to your class, unlocking talent milestones, capstones, gear, zones and achievements between runs.

Where are saves stored in Azeroth Survivors?

Progress persists between runs (talent XP per class), and the game has built-in save-file backup via slash commands and shortcuts. In the browser, saves live in your browser profile for the site you play on — clearing that site's data can wipe them.

Can I play with friends?

Yes — co-op for up to 5 players in one run, in the browser version, since game v0.114.0.
