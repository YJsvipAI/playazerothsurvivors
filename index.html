Azeroth Survivors is a free browser roguelite that dropped in October 2026 and blew up across YouTube, Reddit and a Discord that now counts 113,690 members (public Discord API, 2026-11-10). It runs on the look and spells of World of Warcraft Classic — the 1.12 client era — and a from-scratch open-source engine port (Rust + Bevy, Apache-2.0) exists on GitHub alongside the official build. A run is simple to start and hard to master: you only move, your class spells fire themselves, and every level-up is a three-card draft that reshapes your build.

Straight answers

Azeroth Survivors in three answers

What is it?

A free, fan-made survivors-style roguelike built on the look and spells of World of Warcraft Classic (1.12). Your spells cast themselves — you focus on positioning, drafting upgrades and beating the boss schedule.

Is it free?

Yes. No payment, no account: you press Play at the developer's official site and you are in. An optional support link exists; there is no paywall and no Steam page right now (checked 2026-11-10).

How many classes?

Nine — Hunter, Warlock, Mage, Paladin, Warrior, Shaman, Priest, Druid, Rogue — across 8 races and 13 zones. Hunter, Warlock and Mage currently top the community tier list.

How a run works

Three steps to your first win

Pick a hero

Choose a race, class and zone. Racials matter — Gnomes get luck, Orcs get Blood Fury, Undead cheat death. New to the game? Paladin or Warrior is the easiest way to finish your first zone.

Move and dodge

Steer with WASD while your spells cast themselves. Collect the experience wisps and gold the dead leave behind, and kite hordes inside your auto-cast range.

Draft your build

Every level-up offers three loot-colored cards — new spells, higher ranks or passive buffs. Bosses arrive on a schedule and enrage below 30% health; the final boss ends the zone, and every run pays talent XP that grows your class between runs.

FAQ

Azeroth Survivors FAQ — 21 questions

What is Azeroth Survivors?

A free, fan-made survivors-style roguelike set in the look and spells of World of Warcraft Classic (client 1.12). You pick one of 9 classes, move with WASD while your spells cast themselves, draft one of three loot-colored upgrade cards on every level-up, and beat a boss schedule that ends with the zone's final boss. It is made by a developer known as Aethus and is not an official Blizzard game.

Is Azeroth Survivors free? Do I need an account?

Yes, it is free. The browser version at the developer's official site wow.aethus.pl asks for no payment and no account to play (the hero picker shows an optional Buy-me-a-coffee support link). There is no Steam page at the time of writing (checked 2026-11-10).

Who made Azeroth Survivors?

A developer known as Aethus, who publishes the game at wow.aethus.pl, with updates and bug reports on the official Discord (113,690 members as of 2026-11-10). An open-source engine port in Rust + Bevy (Apache-2.0) also exists on GitHub under OutThisLife/azeroth-survivors-re.

Is Azeroth Survivors an official Blizzard game?

No. It is a fan project: not made, endorsed or sponsored by Blizzard Entertainment. Azeroth and World of Warcraft are trademarks of Blizzard Entertainment, Inc. This site is a fan guide and is not affiliated with Blizzard or with Aethus.

Do I need World of Warcraft installed, or a WoW subscription?

No. The browser and Windows versions are self-contained — you do not need a Blizzard account, a WoW subscription, or any game files from Blizzard.

How many classes does Azeroth Survivors have?

Nine: Hunter, Warlock, Mage, Paladin, Warrior, Shaman, Priest, Druid and Rogue. See our builds hub and tier list for what each class plays like.

How many zones are there?

Thirteen, unlocking in order from Elwynn Forest to Naxxramas. Each zone ends with a scheduled final boss — in Elwynn Forest, Edwin VanCleef arrives at 16:00 (as of game v0.142.0).

What do players also call this game?

Many players call it WoW Survivors. You will also see Warcraft Survivors, Survivors of Azeroth, and the singular Azeroth Survivor. They all mean the same game.

How do you spell it — Azeroth Survivors?

The correct spelling is Azeroth Survivors — that's what we use across this site. Common misspellings include azertoh survivor, azeroth survivers and azeroth surviors; if you searched one of those, this is the game you're looking for.

Where can I play Azeroth Survivors?

In your browser at the developer's official site, wow.aethus.pl (needs a WebGPU-capable browser such as current Chrome, Edge or Safari). An official Windows client is linked from the same official site. This guide links official sources only.

Is there a Windows download?

Yes — an official Windows client is linked from the official site wow.aethus.pl. Community guides recommend it when the browser build is full or slow, and it also enables offline play.

Is the Azeroth Survivors download safe?

Download only from the official site. We never host game files and we do not link re-packed .rar or .torrent copies, which are unofficial and can be modified. The game is free — any page asking you to pay is not the official one.

Why does the game say Too many people are playing?

The official game server is at capacity. The notice clears on its own: reload the page and press Play again a little later, or use the official Windows client instead. Our troubleshooting page has the full checklist.

Why is my screen black or the game not loading?

The game draws its world with WebGPU. A black screen usually means your browser lacks WebGPU or hardware acceleration is off: update Chrome, Edge or Safari, enable hardware acceleration, or use the Windows client. Full fixes are on our troubleshooting page.

What does WebGPU found no usable graphics card mean?

Your browser could not create a WebGPU device from your graphics card. Update your GPU drivers and your browser, make sure hardware acceleration is enabled, and on Linux Chrome enable the WebGPU flag. If your GPU is too old for WebGPU, play the Windows client instead.

Can I play Azeroth Survivors on mobile?

There is no official mobile app. The browser version needs WebGPU, and mobile browser support for it is inconsistent — a desktop browser is currently the most reliable way to play. Community guides also cover Steam Deck.

Is Azeroth Survivors multiplayer?

Yes. Since game v0.114.0, up to 5 players can share one run in co-op in the browser version.

How long is one run?

It depends on the zone. In Elwynn Forest, the first zone, the final boss Edwin VanCleef arrives at 16:00; after he dies you can keep going in endless mode.

Is it related to Vampire Survivors?

Only by genre: it is a survivors-style roguelike — the genre Vampire Survivors popularized — with World of Warcraft-flavored spells, which is why many players call it WoW Survivors. It is an independent fan project with no connection to Vampire Survivors' developers.

Where are my saves stored?

Progress persists between runs — every run pays talent experience to your class. The game includes save-file backup via built-in slash commands and shortcuts. In the browser, your save lives in that browser's profile for the site you play on, so clearing site data can wipe it.

What is the current version of Azeroth Survivors?

v0.143.0 as of 2026-11-10. The game updated rapidly through October 2026 — from v0.114 to v0.142 within about a month — so version-dependent facts on this site are re-checked each patch.
