Each entry carries its source. The pace is the headline: v0.114 to v0.142 landed inside one month in October 2026.

Update cadence

How fast the game moves

October 2026 was the update storm: the game climbed from v0.114 to v0.142 in roughly a month — a pace most games reserve for years — and v0.143.0 followed in early November. Version bumps have landed every few days at times, which is why version-dependent facts (talent point totals, zone counts, co-op limits) are re-checked on this site each patch rather than set in stone.

How we track

What gets listed here, and what doesn't

This log only lists milestones with a source we can point at. Older intermediate builds (v0.92–v0.113 and similar gaps) were not individually documented by the sources we check, so they stay out rather than being guessed at — the fan wiki's patch-notes section is the place that fills those gaps, and the official Discord is where new builds surface first. When a new version lands and community sources confirm it, this page updates and the rest of the site's version-dependent facts get re-checked against it.

Playing on an older client? The Windows client and the browser build on the official site always serve the current version — see our download guide for both channels.

Patch notes FAQ

What is the latest Azeroth Survivors version?

v0.143.0 as of 2026-11-10. The game patched rapidly through October 2026 — v0.114 to v0.142 landed within about a month — and version-dependent guides are re-checked each patch.

How often does Azeroth Survivors update?

Fast and irregular. October 2026 alone carried the game from v0.114 to v0.142 — roughly a dozen versions in a month — and v0.143.0 followed in early November. The official Discord is the fastest place to see a new build land.

Where are the official Azeroth Survivors patch notes?

There is no dedicated official patch-notes page at the time of writing. Changes are announced through the official Discord and show up in the in-game version number; community trackers and the fan wiki's patch-notes section fill in the gaps. This page curates the milestones that have a source.
