WoW Survivors

One game, many names — WoW Survivors is Azeroth Survivors, free in your browser.

Play Free Online How to Play

If you searched "WoW Survivors", you are looking for Azeroth Survivors: the free browser roguelite by developer Aethus that wraps survivors-style runs in World of Warcraft Classic's 1.12-era look and spells. The nickname is so common that it is one of the game's official alternate names — along with Warcraft Survivors and Survivors of Azeroth. Same game, same developer, same nine classes.

The names

One game, many names

The game's official title is Azeroth Survivors. Players shorten or reskin the name constantly, and every variant below lands on the same game by Aethus:

Name in the wildWhat it refers to
WoW SurvivorsThe most common nickname — from the World of Warcraft Classic art direction and spells
Warcraft SurvivorsSame game, same nickname logic; Warcraft instead of WoW
Survivors of AzerothA word-order swap of the real title that means the same game

It is not an official Blizzard game, and it has no connection to Vampire Survivors beyond the genre both helped popularize: the survivors-style roguelike.

What you actually play

Nine classes, thirteen zones, one boss schedule

Pick one of nine classes — Hunter, Warlock, Mage, Paladin, Warrior, Shaman, Priest, Druid or Rogue — move with WASD while your spells cast themselves, and draft one of three loot-colored upgrade cards on every level-up. Thirteen zones unlock in order from Elwynn Forest to Naxxramas, each ending with a scheduled final boss: in Elwynn Forest, Edwin VanCleef arrives at 16:00. Since game v0.114.0, up to five players can share one run in co-op.

Our class builds cover all nine heroes, the tier list ranks them for new players, and the patch notes and version history tracks how fast the game moves (v0.143.0 as of 2026-11-10).

FAQ

WoW Survivors FAQ

Is WoW Survivors the same game as Azeroth Survivors?

Yes. WoW Survivors is the name many players use for Azeroth Survivors, the free fan-made survivors-style roguelike by developer Aethus. It borrows the look and spells of World of Warcraft Classic (1.12), which is where the nickname comes from — but it is not an official Blizzard game.

Is WoW Survivors free? Do I need an account?

Yes, it is free with no account. Play in your browser at the developer's official site wow.aethus.pl (needs a WebGPU-capable browser such as current Chrome, Edge or Safari), or grab the official Windows client linked from the same site. There is no Steam page at the time of writing (2026-11-10, game v0.143.0).

Do I need World of Warcraft, a Blizzard account or a subscription to play?

No. WoW Survivors (Azeroth Survivors) is a self-contained fan game — you do not need World of Warcraft installed, a Blizzard account, or any subscription. Azeroth and World of Warcraft are trademarks of Blizzard Entertainment, Inc.; this fan project is not made or endorsed by Blizzard.
